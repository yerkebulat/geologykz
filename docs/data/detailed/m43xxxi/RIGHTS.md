# Source rights and provenance

This folder contains scan-derived geological data, not original software.

Source: [M-43-XXXI, Geological Map of the USSR, Karaganda series, 1:200,000](https://www.geokniga.org/maps/44362), South Kazakhstan Geological Administration, editor G. Ts. Medoev. The catalog dates it 1959; the map prints 1956. Scan via geokniga.org.

Geokniga's notice reads:
> Все права на материалы принадлежат исключительно их авторам или законным правообладателям. Все материалы предоставляются исключительно для ознакомления.

Rights remain with the authors or lawful rightsholders; the materials are provided for familiarisation. No explicit open redistribution license was found. Availability on Geokniga is not evidence of public-domain or open-license status. Attribution is not a grant of rights. These tiles, polygons and legend transcriptions are excluded from any blanket license applicable to repository software; no additional license to the underlying map is asserted here.

See `provenance.json` for original download URLs, sizes and SHA256 hashes. Original JPG/TIFF scans are deliberately not committed. `georef.json` records manually read control points, provisional datum assumptions and residuals. White Q3-4 areas are mapped geology, not no-data. Colors are experimental classifications, not authoritative unit boundaries. Similar swatches produce multiple candidate units, including γP / γπP?.
